Our Journey & Concept

Built on Friendship, Driven by Passion

Estcom started in 2019 at San Nicolas (right next to Kolets). It began as a humble garage setup when the founder, Sir Edmon Comia, was encouraged by friends who wanted a cozy hangout space for drinks and finger foods (pulutan).

In 2025, Estcom expanded to Wawa, powered by a dedicated team of 5 staff members committed to serving exceptional food and great vibes.
